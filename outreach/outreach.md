# Neighbor-to-neighbor outreach

Send manually, personalize it, and respect a no. Never bulk text or follow up after an opt-out. Confirm the listing's phone is appropriate for business texts. Samples are not leads.

## First text

This template is under 320 characters (with literal placeholders). Generated messages replace the placeholders; check length if the business name or URL is unusually long.

> Hi {name}! I'm Abhinav. I made a website preview for you: {link} — $200 one-time includes setup + 1 year of edits. Pay only if you like it, via Cash App $absubedi. Interested?

`node build.js` generates `outreach/messages.txt` with the same copy for each business, using `PAGES_BASE`. Never send a message labeled FICTIONAL SAMPLE.

## One gentle follow-up

Send once, several business days later, only if appropriate:

> Hi {name}, Abhinav here — just checking that the website preview reached you: {link}. No pressure at all. If it’s useful, I can answer questions or adjust the details. If not, no worries — I won’t follow up again.

## Common replies

**“Is this a subscription?”**
No — $200 once covers the site, setup, and one year of small edits. Hosting on GitHub Pages is free, subject to GitHub’s terms. An optional domain is billed at its actual cost; optional ongoing edits are $15/month, never automatic.

**“What if I don’t like it?”**
No payment needed. It’s an unofficial preview. Tell me what you’d change, or I can take it down — either is fine.

**“What’s included in edits?”**
Small changes to text, hours, prices, contact information, and owner-supplied photos. New features, new pages, branding, booking systems, and a redesign need a separate quote. We’ll agree on scope and turnaround in writing first.

**“We already have Instagram / Google Maps.”**
That’s great — this gives people one simple place for your hours, services, and directions, and can link to your existing profiles. No need to replace what’s working.

**“Can I use my own domain / own the site?”**
Yes. You get the site files, and I can help move them into a GitHub account you control. A custom domain is optional and registered in your name, at cost.

**“How do I pay?”**
Only after you’ve reviewed and approved the details: Cash App **$absubedi**, $200 one-time. I’ll confirm the agreed scope, record the payment, remove the preview ribbon, and help with setup. The one-year small-edit period begins on the agreed launch date.

**“Is this really my business?”**
It’s an independent, unofficial preview built from public information, not a claim to represent your business. Please verify everything. I won’t invent reviews or imply your approval. I can remove it on request.

**“Not interested / stop texting.”**
Understood, thanks for letting me know. I won’t contact you again. Record the opt-out and remove the preview if requested.
